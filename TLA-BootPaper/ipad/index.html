Choose a TLA background for your iPad.

Your iPad starts with the TLA Classic background. Pick any of these to use instead.

Touch and hold the picture, then choose Save to Photos.

  1. Tap Save to Photos above, then Save Image.
  2. Open Photos, open the picture, tap Share, then Use as Wallpaper.
  3. Tap Add, then Set as Wallpaper Pair to use it on the Lock Screen and Home Screen.
